---
id: N11
title: Swap when vowels swamp the rack
tags: vowel_heavy, swap
summary: If every play leaves you with a vowel-heavy rack and scores little, a swap can reset the rack.
---

A swap scores nothing, so only choose it when the best play is small and the leave stays poor. Throw back most of the vowels, and keep one good vowel with your best consonants.
