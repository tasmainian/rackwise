---
id: N24
title: Cash in the blank late in the game
tags: blank_kept
summary: Near the end there are few turns left to use all seven tiles, so the blank is worth less and should be played for points.
---

As the bag empties, the chance of a seven-tile play shrinks. Holding the blank into the last turns often means it scores nothing. Use it while it can still earn a good score.
