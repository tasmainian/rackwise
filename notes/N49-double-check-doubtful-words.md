---
id: N49
title: Double-check doubtful words
tags: invalid_play
summary: When a word you played was flagged as not valid, check it before playing it again, because the word lists used by games can differ.
---

Different games use different word lists. If the coach's list rejected your word, it may be valid in your game but not in that list, or the reverse. Learn which words are safe in the game you play.
