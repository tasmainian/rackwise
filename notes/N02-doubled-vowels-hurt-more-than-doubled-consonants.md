---
id: N02
title: Doubled vowels hurt more than doubled consonants
tags: duplicates, vowel_heavy
summary: Two I's or two U's are among the hardest pairs to hold, while a doubled E or a doubled S does much less harm.
---

Doubled vowels crowd out the consonants you need to build words, and I and U are the least flexible vowels. A pair of E's or S's still fits many words. Rank which duplicate to keep by how many words it appears in twice.
