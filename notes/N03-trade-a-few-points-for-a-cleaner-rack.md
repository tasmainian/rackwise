id: N03
title: Trade a few points for a cleaner rack
tags: duplicates, low_score
summary: Giving up a couple of points to shed a duplicate often pays back on the next turn.
---

The value of a leave shows up on your next turn, not this one. A play a point or two lower that leaves distinct, flexible letters usually scores more over two turns than the top-scoring play that keeps a pair. Once the gap grows large, take the points.
