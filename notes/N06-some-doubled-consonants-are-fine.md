---
id: N06
title: Some doubled consonants are fine
tags: duplicates, consonant_heavy
summary: A pair of common consonants such as two S's or two T's still fits many words, but awkward pairs like two V's or two W's should go quickly.
---

Judge a duplicate by the letter. Common consonants combine with almost anything, so keeping a pair costs little. Awkward letters rarely appear twice in a word, so a pair of them is close to dead weight.
