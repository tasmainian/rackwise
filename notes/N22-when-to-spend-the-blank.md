---
id: N22
title: When to spend the blank
tags: blank_kept, blank_spent
summary: Spend a blank on a play that uses all seven tiles or scores very well, not on a few extra points.
---

The blank is most valuable as the missing piece of a seven-tile play. Using it for a small gain wastes that potential. Good reasons to spend it are a seven-tile play or a score far above your other options.
