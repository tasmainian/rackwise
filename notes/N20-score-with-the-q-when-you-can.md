title: Score with the Q when you can
tags: q_without_u, premium_missed
summary: The Q scores a lot on a letter multiplier, so a play that puts it on one both clears the rack and scores well.
---

When a spot lets you play the Q on a double or triple letter square, take it. You get a large score and remove the hardest tile from your rack at the same time.
