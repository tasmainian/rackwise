---
id: N07
title: Aim for a balanced leave
tags: vowel_heavy, consonant_heavy
summary: A leave with about two vowels and three or four consonants gives you the most words next turn.
---

Balance matters more than any single letter. The bag holds plenty of both vowels and consonants, so a balanced leave tends to become a balanced rack. Leaves that lean hard either way often lead to a weak next turn.
