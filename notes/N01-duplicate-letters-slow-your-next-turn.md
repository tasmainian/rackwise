---
id: N01
title: Duplicate letters slow your next turn
tags: duplicates
summary: Two of the same letter in your leave cut the number of words you can make next turn, so play one off when it costs little.
---

Most words use each letter once. Keeping two U's, two I's or two V's means your next draw has to repair the rack before you can score well. When two plays score about the same, prefer the one that plays a duplicate away.
