---
id: N08
title: Too many vowels
tags: vowel_heavy
summary: Keeping four or more vowels means drawing into a rack with few words, so play vowels off even for a smaller score.
---

Words need consonants to hold vowels together. With a vowel-heavy leave, most of your draw has to be consonants just to get back to normal. Shedding two or three vowels now is often worth a few points.
